# Status

BranchLab is a deterministic full-state transport reference on branch `codex/research-pilot`.

## Implemented

- `src/branchlab/transport.py`: validates multizone inputs, constructs the linear concentration generator, propagates piecewise-constant cleaner controls with `scipy.linalg.expm`, integrates per-room concentration through an augmented linear system, samples exact histories for plotting, and calculates schedule clean-air budgets.
- `src/branchlab/experiment.py`: defines the synthetic A–B–C case, branches independently into a no-cleaner reference and all nine two-half schedules, reports per-room and mean integrated concentrations plus improvements, identifies the best fixed and best overall schedule separately, and replaces the CSV and PNG outputs on each run.
- `tests/test_transport.py`: focused physical, numerical, branching, symmetry, budget, and independent-reference checks.
- `results/transport.csv` and `results/transport.png`: small reproducible example outputs generated by the experiment.

The model keeps observed or supplied inputs (initial full state and synthetic parameters), interventions (cleaner schedule), and calculated deterministic state trajectories/results separate. No inferred state or measured ground truth is present.

## Verification

Verification with `.venv\Scripts\python.exe` passes all seven focused tests: exchange-only mass conservation, isolated-room analytic exponential decay, concentration nonnegativity within numerical tolerance, equal active schedule budgets, branch-input independence, A/C mirror symmetry, and the supplied fixed-placement losses. The experiment completed successfully, produced 40 CSV records (10 branches for each of four separate cases), and generated a visually inspected 3240×1620 figure. Source/test compilation and `git diff --check` also pass.

## Unresolved and out of scope

- Parameters are synthetic and have no measured-data provenance or calibration.
- The fully observed reference has no observation process, hidden-state inference, uncertainty, continuing source, multiple particle classes, time-varying exchange, nonlinear physics, or safety interpretation.
- The well-mixed, fixed-exchange, constant-cleaner assumptions need replacement or validation before any real-building use.
- The current exhaustive comparison is specific to one cleaner, three rooms, two equal operating periods, and two stated horizons; it is not a general optimizer.
