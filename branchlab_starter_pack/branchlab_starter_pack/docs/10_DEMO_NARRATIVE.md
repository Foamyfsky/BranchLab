# Demonstration Narrative

This is a planning scaffold, not the final script.

## Problem

Connected systems often react in delayed and unintuitive ways. A local improvement can move pressure
elsewhere. Existing maps show the current network; static educational diagrams do not let users
fork reality.

## Demonstration arc

1. Open Sydney Pulse and identify that network and schedule are real while demand is synthetic.
2. Play the healthy baseline.
3. Reach the event surge and show pressure accumulating.
4. Pause before the collapse.
5. Apply an apparently reasonable service-frequency intervention.
6. Fork the world.
7. Run baseline and child side-by-side.
8. Show that the origin improves but a transfer station becomes worse.
9. Open first divergence and causal lens.
10. Open Math Lens for one queue update.
11. Show paired uncertainty rather than a single lucky trajectory.
12. Create or preview a second intervention.
13. Export/show the world tree.
14. Briefly show where Codex accelerated implementation and where GPT-5.6 is used.

## Demo rule

Every visual and numerical claim in the video must be reproducible through the reset scenario.
